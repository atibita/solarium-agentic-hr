New Hire Onboarding Guide

Document ID: SOL-HR-104  |  Version 2.5  |  Effective: January 1, 2026  |  Owner: Director of People Operations  |  Classification: Internal — New Hires, Managers, and Onboarding Buddies

Welcome to Solarium. This guide walks through what happens before your start date, your first week, and your first 90 days, plus who to contact along the way. Your manager and your assigned "onboarding buddy" (a peer outside your direct reporting line) are your two main points of contact if anything here is unclear.

1. Before Your Start Date (Pre-boarding)

WhoWhat happens
People OperationsSends your offer paperwork, background check consent (where applicable), and a welcome email with your start date, first-day logistics, and this guide, at least 5 business days before start.
IT OperationsProvisions your accounts and ships your equipment package (see SOL-OPS-202) to arrive by your start date; you'll get a tracking link once it ships.

2. Day 1

  • Log in for the first time using the credentials emailed to your personal email address the evening before.
  • Complete required Day 1 training: Data Security Basics (SOL-SEC-401 overview), Workplace Conduct (SOL-HR-105 overview), and benefits enrollment walkthrough.
  • Meet your manager (1:1) and your onboarding buddy.
  • Attend New Hire Orientation (company-wide, held every Monday) covering Solarium's mission, org structure, and how the Policy Corpus is organized (see SOL-GOV-000).
  • Set up your workstation and confirm your equipment arrived correctly; file an IT ticket immediately if anything is missing or damaged.

3. First Week

Your manager will schedule most of these; if something on this list hasn't been scheduled by Wednesday of your first week, ask your onboarding buddy or People Operations to help chase it down.

  • 1:1 introductions with immediate teammates
  • Team overview session: current projects, tools, and where to find documentation
  • Benefits enrollment deadline reminder — see Section 6 below, elections are due within 30 days of hire
  • Access verification: confirm you can reach the systems your role requires; report any access gaps to IT immediately rather than working around them
  • Review of your role's specific goals for the first 30/60/90 days with your manager

4. The 30/60/90-Day Plan

First 30 Days: Learn

Focus on understanding the team, tools, and how work gets done. Most roles have no expectation of independent output yet — ask questions liberally. Your manager should give you a written (even if brief) set of 30-day goals by the end of your first week.

Days 31–60: Contribute

Begin taking on real, scoped work with regular check-ins. This is a good time to raise early feedback about tools, documentation gaps, or process friction — you have a uniquely fresh perspective that's hardest to recover later.

Days 61–90: Operate

By day 90, most roles expect a level of independent ownership appropriate to the role's seniority. Your manager will hold a formal 90-day check-in covering how onboarding went, any remaining gaps, and goals for the next quarter. This is a two-way conversation — feedback about the onboarding process itself is explicitly welcomed and gets routed back to People Operations to improve the process for future hires.

If at any point in your first 90 days something feels unclear, under-resourced, or different from what was described when you were hired, raise it with your manager or People Operations promptly. Early misalignment is much easier to fix than misalignment discovered at month six.

6. Benefits Enrollment Window

You have 30 days from your hire date to elect health, dental, vision, and retirement plan contributions (see SOL-HR-103, the Benefits Guide, for full plan details). If you miss the window without a qualifying life event, you'll need to wait until the next open enrollment period, so don't leave this until day 29. Retirement plan enrollment (with the default contribution rate) happens automatically if you don't make an active election, but health plan elections do not default — you must actively enroll or you will not have health coverage until the next enrollment window.

8. Intern Addendum

Interns follow this same guide with two adjustments: (1) floating holiday and PTO accrual are pro-rated per SOL-HR-101 Section 9, and (2) the 30/60/90-day plan is compressed to match internship length, with your manager setting milestones appropriate to your program's duration during your first week.
