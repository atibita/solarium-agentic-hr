# Purpose

This policy lists Solarium's company-paid holidays, explains how holidays interact with PTO and remote work, and describes how holiday observance varies for employees outside the United States. It should be read alongside SOL-HR-101 (PTO and Leave Policy).

# 2. Floating Holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays above, every regular employee receives **2 floating holidays** per calendar year to use for religious, cultural, or personal observances not on the standard calendar (for example, Diwali, Eid al-Fitr, Lunar New Year, or a personal milestone).

- Floating holidays are prorated for employees who start mid-year: 1 floating holiday if you start between July 1 and September 30, 0 if you start on or after October 1.
- Floating holidays must be requested through the PTO system at least 5 business days in advance where practical, following the same approval process as PTO.
- Unused floating holidays follow the same carryover rule as PTO described in SOL-HR-101 (up to 5 days may roll into the next year; the rest is forfeited absent a legal requirement to the contrary).
- Floating holidays do not accrue — the full allotment is granted at the start of the year (or pro-rated at hire, as above).

# 3. Holidays That Fall on a Weekend

When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed the following Monday. The table in Section 1 already reflects this adjustment for 2026.

# 4. Winter Break Office Closure

Solarium closes company-wide from December 24 through December 31 each year. This is a paid closure, not a requirement to use PTO, for all regular employees. Employees in roles that must maintain coverage during this period (for example, security on-call, critical infrastructure, and select customer support rotations) will be scheduled in advance by their manager and receive **holiday premium pay or a substitute day off**, per Section 6.

# 5. Non-U.S. Locations

Employees based outside the United States follow the public holiday calendar for their country or state of employment, published each November for the following year by the local People Operations partner, not the U.S. list above. Typical differences include:

- **Country-specific statutory holidays** (e.g., Canada Day, King's Birthday, national independence days) replace the U.S. list entirely for employees in that country.
- **Total paid holiday count** may be higher or lower than the U.S. total depending on local statutory minimums; Solarium will always meet or exceed the local statutory minimum.
- **Winter Break** is still offered globally as a company-wide closure where feasible; in countries where December 24–31 is a normal working period, an equivalent block of days is designated locally and communicated by HR.

Your local calendar is published on the internal policy portal under "Regional Holiday Calendars" and is the authoritative source if it differs from anything in this document.

# 6. Working on a Company Holiday

If your manager asks you to work on a scheduled company holiday because your role requires coverage:

1. Your manager must confirm the request in writing (email or ticket) in advance, except in a genuine emergency.
2. You choose, in agreement with your manager, either (a) 1.5x your normal hourly-equivalent pay for the hours worked that day (exempt employees receive a discretionary bonus of equivalent value), or (b) a substitute paid day off to be taken within 60 days.
3. Time actually worked on a holiday does not count against your PTO balance.

# 7. Holidays During Approved Leave

If a company holiday falls within a period of approved PTO or another paid leave, the holiday is counted as a holiday, not as a PTO day — it is not deducted from your PTO balance. If a holiday falls within a period of unpaid leave, see SOL-HR-101 Section 7 for how holiday pay is (or is not) applied during unpaid leave.

# 8. Religious Accommodation Beyond Floating Holidays

Floating holidays are intended to cover most observance needs, but if they're insufficient for your religious practice, you may request additional unpaid time or a schedule swap as a religious accommodation. Submit this request to People Operations, who will work with your manager on a reasonable accommodation consistent with applicable law. This process is separate from, and does not reduce, your floating holiday allotment.

# Frequently Referenced Numbers

- U.S. fixed holidays in 2026: **12 days** (including 6-day Winter Break)
- Floating holidays per year: **2 days**
- Advance notice for floating holiday requests: **5 business days** where practical
- Floating holiday carryover cap: **5 days**, same as PTO
- Holiday pay premium for required holiday work: **1.5x** or a substitute day off within **60 days**
