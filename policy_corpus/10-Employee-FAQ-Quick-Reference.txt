HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a fast-answer index, not a replacement for the full policies. Each
answer below gives you the short version and points to the source document and
section so you can check the full rule before making a decision that matters
(e.g., leave timing, large expenses, security exceptions). If a short answer
here ever conflicts with the full policy document, the full policy controls —
report the mismatch to governance@solarium.example so we can fix this FAQ.


SECTION 1 — PTO, HOLIDAYS, AND LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How much PTO do I get?
A: 15 days/year (0-2 years tenure), 20 days/year (3-5 years), 25 days/year
   (6+ years), accruing monthly. See SOL-HR-101 Section 2.

Q: How much PTO can I carry over into next year?
A: Up to 5 days. Anything above that is forfeited on Dec 31 unless your state
   requires otherwise. See SOL-HR-101 Section 4.

Q: Do I get paid out for unused PTO when I leave?
A: Where state law requires it, yes, in full. Where it doesn't, Solarium pays
   out up to 10 days as a matter of policy. See SOL-HR-101 Section 4.

Q: How much notice do I need to give for PTO?
A: 2 weeks for anything over 3 consecutive days; 5 business days for shorter
   requests, where practical. See SOL-HR-101 Section 3.

Q: How does sick leave work, and is it different from PTO?
A: Yes, separate bucket. Accrues at 1 hour per 30 hours worked, capped at 72
   hours/year. No doctor's note needed for absences of 3 days or fewer.
   See SOL-HR-101 Section 5.

Q: How much parental leave do I get?
A: 12 weeks at 100% pay, available from day one of employment, for all
   parents regardless of gender, birth/adoptive/foster. See SOL-HR-101
   Section 6.

Q: What if someone in my family dies?
A: 5 paid days for immediate family, 2 paid days for extended family or a
   close friend, usable non-consecutively within 60 days. See SOL-HR-101
   Section 7.

Q: What US holidays does Solarium observe in 2026?
A: 12 paid holiday days including a Dec 24-31 office-wide Winter Break, plus
   2 floating holidays per year. Non-US employees follow a local calendar.
   See SOL-HR-102.

Q: A holiday falls during my vacation — do I lose a PTO day?
A: No. The day counts as a holiday, not PTO. See SOL-HR-102 Section 7.


SECTION 2 — REMOTE WORK AND FLEXIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Can I work from home permanently?
A: Depends on your role's assigned work model (Remote / Hybrid / Office-based),
   set when the role is created. See SOL-OPS-201 Section 1 and 3.

Q: What are core hours?
A: At least 4 hours of overlap with your team's published core hours
   (typically 10am-3pm team time). Outside that, your schedule is flexible.
   See SOL-OPS-201 Section 2.

Q: Do I get money for setting up a home office?
A: Yes — a one-time $750 setup stipend within your first 60 days, plus an
   ongoing $50/month for internet and utilities. See SOL-OPS-201 Section 4.

Q: Can I work from another country for a few weeks?
A: Short trips within your home country under 30 days: just tell your
   manager. International or 30+ day trips: submit a Temporary Work Location
   request at least 10 business days ahead — this is a tax/legal
   requirement. See SOL-OPS-201 Section 5.

Q: I'm hybrid — how many days do I have to be in the office?
A: Set by your manager, typically 2-3 days/week, communicated at least 2
   weeks before it changes. See SOL-OPS-201 Section 9.


SECTION 3 — EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How long do I have to submit an expense?
A: 30 days. After 60 days it needs Controller approval and may be denied.
   See SOL-FIN-301 Section 2.

Q: Do I need a receipt for every expense?
A: Only required for expenses over $25, and it must be itemized, not just a
   card statement line. See SOL-FIN-301 Section 2.

Q: What's the daily meal limit while traveling?
A: $75/day for business travel meals. See SOL-FIN-301 Section 3.

Q: Can I fly business class?
A: Only for international flights over 9 hours, and it requires VP-level
   approval. See SOL-FIN-301 Section 4.

Q: I accidentally used my corporate card for something personal — am I in
   trouble?
A: Not if you report it promptly and reimburse Solarium through the process
   Finance provides. It's treated as a mistake, not a violation, if you fix
   it quickly. See SOL-FIN-301 Section 5.

Q: How much can my manager approve without escalating?
A: Up to $2,500 per single expense. Directors up to $10,000, VPs up to
   $50,000. See SOL-FIN-301 Section 8.


SECTION 4 — DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: I think I clicked a phishing link — what do I do?
A: Report it immediately using the "Report Phishing" button or email
   security@solarium.example. Reporting promptly is never itself a
   disciplinary event. See SOL-SEC-401 Section 8.

Q: My laptop was stolen — what's the deadline to report it?
A: Within 1 hour of discovery, or as soon as possible via the 24/7 hotline
   after hours. See SOL-SEC-401 Section 4.3.

Q: Can I paste customer data into an AI chat tool to help draft something?
A: Never paste Restricted-tier data (customer PII, payment data, credentials,
   health data) into any AI tool unless it's specifically certified for
   Restricted data on the tools portal. See SOL-SEC-401 Section 6.

Q: Do I need MFA on everything?
A: Yes, on every account that supports it — email, SSO, VPN, cloud consoles.
   SMS-based MFA is discouraged and disallowed for Restricted systems.
   See SOL-SEC-401 Section 3.

Q: Can I use my personal laptop to check work email?
A: Yes, via the approved mobile/work-profile app, but personal devices can't
   be used to access Confidential or Restricted data without a documented
   BYOD exception. See SOL-SEC-401 Section 4.2.


SECTION 5 — BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: When do my benefits start?
A: Day one of employment, for anyone regularly scheduled 20+ hours/week.
   See SOL-HR-103 Section 2.

Q: How long do I have to enroll?
A: 30 days from your hire date. Health coverage does NOT default — you must
   actively enroll or you have no coverage until the next window. Retirement
   auto-enrolls at 4% if you don't act. See SOL-HR-103 Section 2 and
   SOL-HR-104 Section 6.

Q: What's the 401(k) match?
A: 100% match on the first 4% you contribute, then 50% on the next 2% —
   effectively up to 5% match on a 6% contribution. Vests immediately.
   See SOL-HR-103 Section 4.

Q: Is there a wellness stipend?
A: Yes, $75/month for gym, fitness, or mental health apps, submitted like a
   normal expense. Plus 1 paid wellness day/year separate from PTO.
   See SOL-HR-103 Section 6.

Q: Can I change my health plan mid-year?
A: Generally no, unless you have a qualifying life event (marriage, birth,
   loss of other coverage, etc.), which opens a 30-day window. This is an
   IRS rule, not a Solarium-specific restriction. See SOL-HR-103 Section 10.


SECTION 6 — ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: When will my equipment arrive?
A: IT ships it to arrive by your start date once your offer is finalized.
   See SOL-HR-104 Section 1 and SOL-OPS-202 Section 2.

Q: What training do I need to complete, and by when?
A: Data Security Basics (Day 1), Workplace Conduct training (first 5 business
   days, then annually), Benefits enrollment (30 days). See SOL-HR-104
   Section 5.

Q: Who do I ask if something in onboarding seems off track?
A: Your manager or your onboarding buddy first; People Operations any time.
   See SOL-HR-104 Section 4.


SECTION 7 — EQUIPMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What do I get when I'm hired?
A: A laptop, monitor, keyboard/mouse, headset, and laptop bag, shipped or
   available for pickup. See SOL-OPS-202 Section 2.

Q: My laptop broke — what happens?
A: File an IT ticket. IT troubleshoots remotely first, then arranges a
   loaner/replacement, typically within 2-5 business days depending on
   location. Accidental damage is fine to report — no penalty for reporting.
   See SOL-OPS-202 Section 6.

Q: Do I have to return my laptop when I leave?
A: Yes — by your last day if local, or within 5 business days by prepaid
   shipping label if remote. See SOL-OPS-202 Section 9.

Q: Can I get ergonomic equipment like a better chair?
A: Yes, up to $300 per employee every 3 years, no special justification
   needed under that amount. See SOL-OPS-202 Section 3.


SECTION 8 — WORKPLACE CONDUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How do I report a harassment or conduct concern?
A: Your manager, another manager you trust, People Operations, the
   confidential ethics hotline, or General Counsel's office — whichever
   channel you're most comfortable with. You never have to go through your
   own manager if they're involved. See SOL-HR-105 Section 5.

Q: Will reporting something get back to the person I reported, or affect me?
A: Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a serious violation,
   independently investigated. A report that isn't ultimately substantiated
   is not treated as a "false report" or held against you. See SOL-HR-105
   Sections 3.3 and 9.

Q: How long does an investigation take?
A: Most conclude within 30 days; you'll get a status update if it runs
   longer. See SOL-HR-105 Section 6.

Q: Is my manager required to report something they overhear, even if I ask
   them not to?
A: Yes — managers cannot handle harassment or discrimination concerns off
   the record. See SOL-HR-105 Section 10.
